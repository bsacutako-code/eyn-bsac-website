About BSAC

Brethren Skills Acquisition Center (BSAC) is an initiative of Church of the Brethren in Nigeria (EYN) LCC Utako, Abuja, dedicated to empowering individuals through comprehensive skills acquisition.

We equip students with high-demand practical skills including Fashion Design, Bakery, Coding, Adult Education, and Graphic Design that create sustainable opportunities for self reliance.

Our commitment is to raise highly skilled professionals capable of excelling and competing globally in their respective industries.
